ARCHYTECTURE

Archytecture is a fictional architecture studio focused on designing modern, sustainable and characterful spaces and homes. For this project, I designed a website that places the studio’s architecture and projects at the centre through a calm and visually driven experience. Rather than relying on large amounts of text and technical information, the design focuses on large-scale imagery, clear typography and a minimalist aesthetic. The goal of the website is to allow visitors to experience Archytecture’s vision and design style, while introducing them to the studio and its projects in an accessible way.

VISUELE IDENTITEIT

I started by defining the visual direction and desired look and feel of the architecture studio. Based on this foundation, I developed a clear page structure and a flexible grid system. I then designed the key screens in Figma, including the homepage, project overview and several project detail pages.
