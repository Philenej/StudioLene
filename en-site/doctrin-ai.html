DoctrinAi

For DoctrinAI, I designed posters that strengthen the brand’s visibility and recognition within secondary education. DoctrinAI offers practical training sessions where teachers learn how to use AI in a safe, effective, and accessible way within their lessons and lesson preparation.

The posters were designed to immediately capture attention while still feeling approachable and trustworthy. Through the use of futuristic visuals, deep blue tones, and clear typography, the design connects to the theme of AI without feeling overly technical or complicated.

The project translates DoctrinAI’s vision into a visual style that resonates with educators and presents AI in a clear and modern way.
