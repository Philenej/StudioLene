AURORA

Aurora is a concept for a health app in which health data is translated into an intuitive and attractive visual experience, inspired by the Northern Lights. Instead of focusing on numbers, graphs, and percentage-based visuals, the emphasis is placed on feeling. The goal of the concept is not to make users analyze their data, but to help them recognize how their body feels.

The visualization responds to three different types of health data: sleep, movement, and heart rate.

The colours of the Northern Lights are based on intuitive associations:

Green — movement and vitality

Blue — rest and sleep

Pink — heartbeats, reflection, and the heart

VISUELE IDENTITEIT

Design challenge: How can we design visualizations that create emotional engagement and motivate users to become more physically active and develop healthier sleep habits?

User testing: Feedback from others showed that the northern lights fascinates people, drawing users into the visualization and sparking their curiosity.
