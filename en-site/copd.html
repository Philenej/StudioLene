The sound of COPD

For the platform Dit is COPD, I designed a website that raises awareness about COPD, a chronical lung disease that affects hundreds of thousands of people in the Netherlands. The website offers a place for information, recognition, and shared experience.

The concept of the website is creative, accessible, and empathetic, with a focus on connecting patients and readers to better understand COPD in a human, accessible manner.
